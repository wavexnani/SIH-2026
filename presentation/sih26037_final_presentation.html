PROBLEM DEFINITION • SLIDE 1 / 6
THE UNSTRUCTURED ROAD CHALLENGE CORE PROBLEM

Indian road environments cannot be represented by rigid lane geometries. High-density mixed traffic, sudden livestock incursions, unmarked village corridors, and informal yielding invalidate classical lane-following autonomy.

1. Lane Absence & Boundary Drift

Faded or non-existent lane markings. Drivable bounds must be synthesized dynamically from open free space.

2. Heterogeneous Road Users

Simultaneous interaction with commercial vehicles, auto-rickshaws, two-wheelers, pedestrians, and cattle.

3. Motion Uncertainty Spikes

Unpredictable cutting and lateral shifts require dynamic uncertainty-expanded spatial safety corridors.

4. Tight Temporal Deadlines

Real-time closed-loop execution budget ≤ 20 ms (50 Hz) for online convex QP trajectory optimization.

Perception Modeling Boundary: Current simulation pipeline employs an analytical Tracked-Object ObservationModel (with injected position/velocity noise, tracking latency, and false positive/negative perturbations). Raw camera/LiDAR hardware fusion represents the next integration stage.

Uncertainty-Expanded Spatial Corridors and Dual Active-Set Predictive Trajectory Optimization
THEORETICAL FOUNDATION • SLIDE 3 / 6
MATHEMATICAL & CONTROL PIPELINE CONVEX CONSTRAINTS
1. DYNAMIC FREE-SPACE EXTRACTION

At each step k across prediction horizon Np=10, extract drivable interval:
y_min(k) ≤ y_ego(k) ≤ y_max(k) derived from road boundaries minus obstacle forbidden zones.

2. SHORT-HORIZON PREDICTION & 2σ UNCERTAINTY EXPANSION

Kinematic projection of agent j with velocity uncertainty:
ŷ_j(τ) = y_j + v_y,j • τ,   δy_unc(τ) = 2 • σ_v • τ

Statistical Note: 2σ expansion corresponds to ≈95.45% coverage under the assumed Gaussian velocity model. This is an engineering heuristic, not a formal probabilistic guarantee.
3. CONSTRAINED RECEDING-HORIZON QP-MPC

Solves convex quadratic program via Hildreth dual active-set procedure:
min_U ½ U' H U + F' U   s.t.   G U ≤ W
Enforcing lateral boundaries, steering limits (±35°), and acceleration bounds [-6.0, +3.0] m/s².

4. INDEPENDENT RUNTIME SAFETY FILTER (LAYER 2)

Evaluates proposed control ucmd using forward trajectory projection and Oriented Bounding Box (OBB) Separating Axis Theorem (SAT) checks. Executes emergency braking if boundary or clearance violations are imminent.

*Clarification: The safety filter is an independent geometric OBB/SAT veto, not a Control Barrier Function (CBF).
