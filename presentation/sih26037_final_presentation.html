Research Landscape: Prior Art, Limitations & Our Engineering Response
Rigorous mapping of peer-reviewed foundations to PS26037 requirements
Research / Resource Established Literature Capability Remaining Limitation for PS26037 Our Integrated Engineering Response
IDD / IDD-3D
Varma et al. (WACV '19) · Dokania ('23) 		Documents unstructured Indian road scenes, heterogeneous vehicle classes, and drivable-area semantic segmentation. Establishes the perception problem benchmark; it is an open-loop dataset, not a closed-loop motion planner. Free-Space Planning: Converts observed drivable space into analytical bounds [ymin(x), ymax(x)] rather than assuming a painted centerline.
Lane-Free Planning
Troullinos, Papageorgiou et al. (IEEE T-IV '23) 		Uses continuous lateral road space and optimal control for vehicle nudging in lane-free automated traffic. Assumes 100% cooperative connected automated vehicles (CAVs); fails under erratic non-connected cattle and pedestrians. Interaction-Aware Adaptation: Explicit FOLLOW / YIELD / OVERTAKE state machine gating dynamic corridor topologies.
LTV / QP-MPC
Falcone et al. (IEEE TCST '07) · Borrelli ('17) 		Provides predictive constrained trajectory and steering optimization suitable for real-time vehicle dynamics. Requires structured reference pathways and convex boundaries supplied by the external planning stack. Corridor QP-MPC: Solves 50 Hz condensed Hildreth QP directly within uncertainty-expanded corridors without lane references.
Runtime Shielding
Alshiekh et al. (AAAI '18) · Gottschalk ('96) 		Separates nominal task optimization from an independent, formal safety-monitoring and override mechanism. General algorithmic principle; vehicle footprint geometry and emergency braking policies must be specifically engineered. Independent OBB/SAT Veto: Exact 2D Oriented Bounding Box geometric collision check overriding MPC with controlled safe stopping.
Remaining Engineering Gap

What Changes When the Lane Fails?

Conventional Assumption PS26037 Reality Our Engineering Response
Lane Centerline Exists Lane markings missing, eroded, or ambiguous in >40% of scenes. Dynamic Free-Space Corridor: Continuous bounds [ymin(x), ymax(x)].
Predictable Agents Sudden cut-ins, informal merging, pedestrians & livestock. Uncertainty Expansion: Lateral corridor inflation (±2σvτ).
Planner Output Trusted Optimization can become infeasible or geometrically unsafe. Independent Safety Veto: OBB/SAT kinematic shield + safe stop.
Closed-Loop Integration

One Unified Engineering Pipeline

1
Observed Agents: Tracked state vector + noise/latency perturbations(ObservationModel.m)
2
Free Space: Dynamic lateral bounds without painted lane centerline(FreeSpaceMap.m)
3
Prediction + Uncertainty: Short-horizon envelopes inflated by velocity variance (±2σvτ)(UncertaintyPredictor.m)
4
Behavioral Intent: Deterministic state machine: MAINTAIN · FOLLOW · YIELD · OVERTAKE
5
CA-CRC + QP-MPC: Topology evaluation (Jleft vs Jright) & 50 Hz Hildreth trajectory QP
6
OBB/SAT Safety Veto: Exact polygonal footprint check overriding unsafe commands(SafetyFilter.m)
Key Architectural Novelty: Uncertainty is injected directly into the planning constraints ([ymin(k) ≤ y(k) ≤ ymax(k)]), not treated merely as a post-processing check.
