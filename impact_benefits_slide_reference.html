INFRASTRUCTURE INDEPENDENCE
Planning without a predefined lane centerline
● DEMONSTRATED IN SIMULATION
“Planning does not require a predefined lane centerline.”

Mechanism: The planner represents locally available drivable space as dynamic spatial bounds rather than treating lane geometry as the primary planning reference.

FIXED LANE ✕ Requires painted markings
DYNAMIC FREE SPACE ✓ Adapts to free corridors
Practical Benefit: Autonomous navigation can be evaluated on roads where lane markings are missing, degraded, or temporarily obstructed.
Designed for lane-poor and unstructured environments.
ADAPTIVE TRAFFIC NEGOTIATION
Traffic interaction changes the maneuver
● DEMONSTRATED IN TESTED SCENARIOS
“Traffic interaction changes the maneuver, not just the trajectory.”

Mechanism: Classifies interaction conditions and evaluates behavior decisions (maintain, follow, yield, overtake) prior to trajectory optimization.

Interaction
➔
Follow / Yield / Overtake
➔
Topology
Practical Benefit: The vehicle adapts its local maneuver to surrounding traffic behavior instead of treating obstacles as static path points.
Evaluates alternative local path topologies (Left ↔ Right) when road space permits.
SAFE DEGRADATION
Fail-safe stopping in non-traversable bottlenecks
● DEMONSTRATED IN SIMULATION
“When no feasible passage exists, the system stops rather than force a path.”

Mechanism: Combines feasibility-aware corridor construction with an independent safety validation layer.

TESTED BOTTLENECK RESULT Wgap < 1.60 m ➔ Standstill
Practical Benefit: Infeasible road geometry becomes a controlled failure condition rather than an instruction to force passage through narrow space.
Demonstrated in tested narrow-gap bottleneck scenarios.
SCALABLE VEHICLE PLATFORM
Planning stack decoupled from future sensing
○ PROPOSED EXTENSION
“Planning remains decoupled from the future sensing platform.”

Mechanism: The planning layer consumes object states, uncertainty and space bounds, allowing future perception suites to evolve independently.

CURRENT MIL: Synthetic / noisy observations
FUTURE SCOPE: Camera + LiDAR + Radar fusion
Practical Benefit: Creates a staged path from algorithm validation in MIL to realistic sensor simulation and vehicle platform integration.
Decoupled architecture enables independent perception development.
